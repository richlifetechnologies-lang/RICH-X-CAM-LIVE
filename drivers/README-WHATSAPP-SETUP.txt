========================================================================
RICH X CAM LIVE - WhatsApp Desktop & Calling Apps Integration Guide
========================================================================

STEP 1: Run the Virtual Device Installer (Once)
-----------------------------------------------
Right-click on:
  install-richx-virtual-devices.bat
and select "Run as administrator".

The installer automatically configures:
  1. [RICHX CAM] - Native DirectShow Virtual Camera Filter
     (Registers richxcam64.dll / UnityCaptureFilter64.dll as "RICHX CAM")
  2. [RICHX MIC] - Virtual Audio Cable Driver
     (Installs bundled VB-CABLE driver and configures "RICHX MIC")

IMPORTANT: Fully close and restart your calling apps (WhatsApp, Zoom, Teams, Discord)
after running the installer so the new devices appear in their hardware device dropdowns.

To remove the devices later, run:
  uninstall-richx-virtual-devices.bat
(as administrator).

STEP 2: Configure Calling Apps (WhatsApp, Zoom, Teams, Discord)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open WhatsApp / Zoom / Teams / Discord / Google Meet.
2. Go to Settings -> Audio & Video (or Calls / Devices).
3. Under "Camera":
   Select "RICHX CAM".
4. Under "Microphone":
   Select "RICHX MIC".

STEP 3: Start your Call in RICH X CAM LIVE
------------------------------------------
1. Launch RICH X CAM LIVE software and activate your license key.
2. Select your persona avatar image and appearance prompt.
3. Click "Start Video + Audio Call" (or "Start Video Only Call").
4. Make your call in WhatsApp, Zoom, Teams, or Discord - your converted real-time
   video avatar and synchronized voice will stream live into your call!

Compatible Applications:
- WhatsApp Desktop & WhatsApp Web
- Telegram Desktop & Web
- Zoom Desktop Client
- Microsoft Teams
- Discord
- Google Meet / Google Chrome / Edge
- Skype / OBS Studio
