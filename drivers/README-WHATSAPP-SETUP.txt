========================================================================
RICH X CAM LIVE - WhatsApp Desktop & Calling Apps Integration Guide
========================================================================

STEP 1: Run the Virtual Device Installer (once)
-----------------------------------------------
Right-click on:
  install-richx-virtual-devices.bat
and select "Run as administrator".

The installer:
  - Downloads and installs the official VB-CABLE virtual audio driver
    (free, from vb-audio.com) if needed, and renames its capture endpoint
    to "RICHX MIC".
  - Guides you through setting up a virtual camera (e.g. OBS Virtual
    Camera) that appears as your video device in calling apps.

IMPORTANT: Restart your calling apps after running the installer so the
new devices appear in their camera/microphone lists.

To remove the devices later, run:
  uninstall-richx-virtual-devices.bat
(as administrator).

STEP 2: Configure WhatsApp (Desktop or Web)
-------------------------------------------
1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Click the gear icon (Settings) -> "Audio & Video" (or "Calls").
3. Under "Camera":
   Select your virtual camera (e.g. "OBS Virtual Camera").
4. Under "Microphone":
   Select "RICHX MIC".

STEP 3: Start your Call in RICH X CAM LIVE
------------------------------------------
1. Launch RICH X CAM LIVE software and activate your license key.
2. Select your persona avatar image and appearance prompt.
3. Click "Start Video + Audio Call" (or "Start Video Only Call").
4. Make your call in WhatsApp - your converted real-time video avatar and
   synchronized voice will stream live into WhatsApp!

Compatible Applications:
- WhatsApp Desktop & WhatsApp Web
- Telegram Desktop & Web
- Zoom Desktop Client
- Microsoft Teams
- Discord
- Google Meet / Google Chrome / Edge
- Skype
